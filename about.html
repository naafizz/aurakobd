Our Story

Since 2019

Slow beauty, rooted in Korean tradition

AURAKO began with a simple belief: that skincare should be a ritual, not a routine. We work with fifteen independent Korean formulation houses — from Jeju Island botanists to fourth-generation ginseng farmers — to bring considered, effective skincare to a global audience.

Every formula we carry is chosen for its ingredient integrity, sensorial experience, and visible results. No filler actives, no greenwashing — just skincare that respects both your skin and your time.
